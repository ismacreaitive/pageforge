1. Your voice sample

A clear recording of you speaking, 5 to 12 seconds is ideal. The AI copies your voice from it. Only the first 12 seconds are used, so start speaking right away and avoid music or noise.

2. The photo

A clear, front-facing photo with the face large in the picture works best.

3. What the avatar will say

Type the script in English (the free voice-cloning Space handles English and Chinese). Leave a blank line between parts. Each part becomes one talking clip, and long parts are split automatically (about 40 words per clip is best).

4. Settings

Fast mode uses pragnakalp/Wav2lip-ZeroGPU first and falls back to the Space above. Natural mode does the opposite.
