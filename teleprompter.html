🎥 How I record

What am I making?
Where I film
  1. Put your phone just above the middle of this screen, back camera facing you: lean it on books behind the screen so the camera peeks over the top edge, or hold it with a clip.
  2. Lift the computer on books so the camera is at your eye level. Sit about 1.5 metres away and make the text bigger with A+.
  3. Turn the phone sideways for YouTube or keep it upright for Shorts.
  4. Open the phone's normal camera app, video mode, and start recording.
  5. Here, press Play (or the Space key) and read. The text sits right under the phone, so you look at the camera.
  6. Stop the phone. Your script is already saved on this computer, so the video maker can use your exact words for the captions.
🗣 Voice-follow on a computer: the computer's own microphone must hear your words. Watch the box at the top that says Heard:. If it shows nothing, speak a little louder, check the language, or plug in an earphone with a microphone. Voice-follow needs internet.
🎙️ Audio only: no camera opens and your face is never shown. The text still moves while you read, and your voice is recorded. Save the audio and drop it into the video maker to build the video from it (stock pictures, captions, cover…). Best for Quran reminders and voice-overs.
